# Fleet Traps and Pitfalls Log

- [Terrain/Shader/GrassShells]: `terrain_grass_shells.gdshader` extrudes 8 shells up to `grass_height = 0.45` (45 cm). When structures or dock pads are placed on terrain, the 45 cm tall grass carpet slices through thin slabs (e.g. 7-8 cm thick pads). Resolution: in `TerrainBuilder.apply_building_pad_flattening()`, grass shell array meshes within the building pad footprint must be updated with `v_fit = 0.0` (`COLOR.r = 0.0`) so grass shells are discarded inside building pads and concrete surfaces.
- [Battle/Buildings/Structure]: Dock pads (`DOCK_PAD_SIZE`) previously had co-planar top faces with their foundation kerb plinth (`pad_y + pad_size.y * 0.5`), causing severe z-fighting and flickering across the shared 7.0m x 9.0m area. Resolution: Kerb plinth must sit slightly below the pad driving surface (e.g. 12 mm lower), and the pad slab thickness should be at least 14 cm with an elevated driving surface so the asphalt pad stands proud of the curb.
- [UI/RosterPicker]: In `roster_picker.gd`, `_bake_thumbnails()` was called in `setup()` prior to the node entering the tree. `if not is_inside_tree(): return` caused the loop to abort before rendering thumbnails and computing stats, leaving cards permanently in the unmeasured `"measuring..."` state. Resolution: Use `await tree_entered`, maintain a static thumbnail and stats cache across screen navigations, and populate preliminary stats immediately from blueprint JSON metadata.
- [Tools/BakeUSGSTopo]: Topo contour lines cannot rely on simple range thresholding (`abs(H - L) < eps`) because steep gradients skip threshold buckets leaving gaps. Resolution: Use discrete quantization boundary detection (`np.floor(H * intervals)`) with gradient differencing (`diff_x | diff_y`), plus dilation for bold index contours (`every 4th contour`). Map world coordinate bounds (`map_half_extents`) must be read from map JSONs to correctly project `surface_zones` (forest woodland green wash and tree stipples) and `obstacles` (rock diagonal hashes).
- [Tools/BakeUSGSTopo/TerrainSculpt]: Custom maps authored in TerrainSculpt (`sculpt_grid`) store floating-point elevation in meters (e.g. -55m to +102m). Casting `(grid * 255).astype(np.uint8)` caused continuous modulo 256 byte-wrapping across negative and >1m values, turning the entire terrain into high-frequency static noise lines. Resolution: Keep `sculpt_grid` as float32, resize with `PIL.Image.fromarray(grid, mode='F')`, and map `props` (trees and rocks) directly to world coordinates.
- [Godot/SubViewport/Camera3D]: In Godot 4, a `Camera3D` placed inside a `SubViewport` will not be used by the viewport unless `camera.current = true` is explicitly assigned, or if `own_world_3d = true` is set. Without this, the viewport falls back to an internal camera at `(0, 0, 0)` looking down `-Z`, causing subjects positioned around the origin or with custom camera offsets to fall outside the camera frustum or clip behind the near plane.
- [Terrain/Water/Submerged]: In `TerrainBuilder`, water exists across three distinct mechanisms: 1) global water table (where `height < water_level - 0.6` on v2/sculpt maps, defaulting to `water_level = -2.0`), 2) painted water rasters (`*_water.png`), and 3) explicit `water_areas` / `shallow_water_areas` bounding boxes. Map bakers and preview schematics must combine all three sources to accurately reflect river deltas, submerged flats, lakes, and channels.
- [UI/HeroView/VehicleGrounding]: `reconstruct_vehicle` assigns `vehicle.position.y = maxf(-lowest, hull_size.y / 2.0)` which already grounds the wheels at parent `y = 0.0`. If a caller then overrides `vehicle.position.y = -v_box.position.y` or sets `vehicle.position = offset`, it cancels out the internal lift or causes tracks to sink below the plinth. Furthermore, wide combat vehicles (missile trucks, heavy siege tanks) can be 5.5m to 8.5m wide, so static narrow lateral offsets (`dx = 3.2m`) cause vehicles to overlap and clip laterally. Resolution: Keep `recon_y = vehicle.position.y` when positioning on a plinth surface (`y = 0.0`), dynamically calculate non-overlapping lateral echelon offsets by accumulating each vehicle's half-width plus a minimum clearance gap (`min_gap = 1.8m`), and widen the staging apron plinth radius (`28m+`).
- [Battle/Units/Movement]: In `unit._apply_movement()`, the destination selection ladder checks `Order.has_destination()`, which only returns `true` for `MOVE` and `ATTACK_MOVE`. Orders that carry an explicit ground position (such as `ATTACK_GROUND`) that need pathing when out of weapon range must be explicitly branched (`elif is_attack_ground_order: destination = current_order.position`) in `_apply_movement`'s destination selection ladder, or the unit falls through to `else` and comes to a dead halt.
- [Godot/Node/TreeAccess]: In Godot 4 headless test scripts or synthetic harnesses where nodes are instantiated detached from an active scene tree, calling `get_node_or_null()` with absolute paths (e.g. `"/root/CursorManager"`) or `get_tree()` throws engine errors (`Can't use get_node() with absolute paths from outside the active scene tree` / `Parameter "data.tree" is null`). Any subsystem or manager accessing `/root/*` or `get_tree()` should guard with `if is_inside_tree():` before querying absolute paths or creating scene tree timers.
- [Godot/CSG/ClippingVisuals]: Attempting runtime Boolean CSG operations (`CSGCombiner3D` with `OPERATION_INTERSECTION` and `CSGMesh3D` children) to visualize clipping intersections of complex kitbash meshes produces severe visual artifacts in Godot 4. Non-manifold meshes, mismatched transforms, and runtime triangulation passes generate disjointed, floating red duplicate shells or offset clone surfaces hovering above the vehicle. Furthermore, allocating and meshing CSG trees on every drag/placement tick imposes heavy CPU overhead. Resolution: Discard runtime CSG meshing entirely. Highlight colliding modules by swapping `material_override` to a shared static `_clipping_material()` on the module's `MeshInstance3D` children while saving and restoring their initial `material_override` via `base_material` node metadata.
